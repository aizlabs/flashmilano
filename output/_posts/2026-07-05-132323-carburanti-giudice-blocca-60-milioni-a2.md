La Guardia di Finanza <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-3">indaga su</button> Outlet Carburanti, una <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-4">società</button> di Segrate, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-5">vicino a</button> Milano. L'Iva è una <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-11">tassa</button> che le <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">aziende</button> pagano sulle <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-13">vendite</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">Secondo</button> gli investigatori, la <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-4">società</button> ha pagato meno Iva <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-15">del dovuto</button>. La <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-4">società</button> vendeva <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-20">benzina</button> e <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-21">gasolio</button>. Prima il <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-1">carburante</button> stava in <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-25">depositi</button> speciali per l'Iva. Gli investigatori dicono che il calcolo era <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-28">troppo basso</button>. Così Outlet Carburanti poteva avere <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-29">prezzi</button> più bassi.

La <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-4">società</button> vendeva in due <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">distributori</button> indipendenti a Segrate. Molte persone chiamano questi <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-30">distributori</button> <button type="button" class="article-term article-term--default" data-term-id="term-31">pompe bianche</button>. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-32">Inoltre</button> vendeva <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-1">carburante</button> ad altre <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-12">aziende</button> del settore. <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-29">Prezzi</button> troppo bassi possono creare problemi agli altri <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-33">venditori</button>.

Il <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-34">blocco</button> <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-9">riguarda</button> i <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-7">soldi</button> che, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-14">secondo</button> gli investigatori, <button type="button" class="article-term" data-term-id="term-35">mancano</button> allo Stato.
